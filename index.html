ResearchPublished work

arXiv preprint · 2026

IndicDB: Benchmarking Multilingual Text-to-SQL Capabilities in Indian Languages

Aviral Dawar, Roshan Karanth, Vikram Goyal, Dhruv Kumar

A Text-to-SQL benchmark built from Indian public data: 20 databases, 237 tables and 15,617 tasks. An Architect–Auditor–Refiner agent pipeline turns denormalised administrative data into relational schemas averaging 11.85 tables per database, with joins up to six deep. Across the LLMs evaluated, execution accuracy drops by 9 points on average from English to Indic-language questions.

ICML 2026 · AI for Law Workshop · poster

AFL-Law: Authority, Framing, and Language Invariance Testing in Large Language Models

Chehak Malhotra, Aviral Dawar

Does an LLM's legal verdict change when you add details that shouldn't matter? We ran 100 Indian Supreme Court cases through five model conditions in English, Hindi, Chinese and Spanish, varying authority cues and persuasive framing. Authority bias reached 37% and framing sensitivity reached 100% in some conditions.
